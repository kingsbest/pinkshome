Mould & damp cleaning

Mould & Damp Cleaning in South-West Wales

Damp Welsh winters and warm, closed-up homes are the perfect conditions for black mould — on walls, ceilings, around windows and along sealant. We clean it properly and treat the surface so it doesn’t come straight back, across Swansea, Carmarthenshire and Pembrokeshire.

What we do

We don’t just paint over mould. We clean it off, treat the surface with an appropriate anti-mould solution, and tell you the likely cause — which, nine times out of ten, is condensation and not enough ventilation. You’ll be left with a clean surface and a clear idea of how to keep it from returning.

Straight talking

What we can and can’t do

We handle surface mould cleaning and treatment, which covers the great majority of household mould. If what you’ve actually got is structural or rising damp — water coming in from outside, or up through the walls — that needs a specialist damp-proofing company, and we’ll tell you straight if that’s what we find rather than treating a symptom.

For landlords

Damp and mould are no longer something a landlord can leave. Under Awaab’s Law and the Renters’ Rights Act, you’re expected to deal with it, and promptly. We turn jobs around quickly and give you before-and-after photos for your records.

Anti-mould treatments that are safe around children and pets, with HEPA-standard cleaning where the job calls for it.
