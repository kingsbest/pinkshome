Jet washing or soft washing?

Jet washing

  • High-pressure water
  • Paving, concrete, patios

Soft washing

  • Low pressure plus a cleaning solution
  • Render, painted walls, delicate surfaces

Jet washing uses high-pressure water and suits hard surfaces like paving, concrete and patios. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution, which is the safer choice for render, painted walls and anything that could be damaged by a high-pressure jet. We’ll tell you which your surfaces need when we quote.
