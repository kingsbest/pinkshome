After builders & post-construction

After Builders & Post-Construction Cleaning in South-West Wales

A finished build looks nothing like a clean one. Fine plaster dust settles into every surface, paint and adhesive cling to glass and floors, and new sanitary ware arrives covered in stickers and grime. We take a site from “work finished” to “ready to hand over” — across Swansea, Carmarthenshire and Pembrokeshire.

What’s included

Most jobs run in two stages, and we’ll tell you which you need.

.01

The builders clean

The first pass once the heavy work is done. We clear construction dust and debris, sweep and vacuum throughout, wipe down surfaces and get the space ready for its final finishes.

.02

The sparkle clean

The detailed final clean before handover: windows and frames inside and out, paint splatter and grout haze removed, adhesive labels and residue off the glass, sanitary ware and sinks polished, floors brought up to their finished state, and switches, sockets and skirting wiped down.

Who we work for

We clean for builders, property developers, shopfitters, landlords and homeowners finishing a renovation or extension. If you’re handing a property to a client, a buyer or a tenant, we’ll make sure it shows the standard of the work that went into it.

Why Pink’s

We’re fully insured, used to working alongside other trades on an active site, and we’ll work to your handover date — including evenings and weekends when a deadline is tight. You get a clear fixed quote before we start, and photos when it’s done.

What it costs

After-builders cleans are priced per site rather than by the hour, because every job is different. Send us a few photos of the space and the rough floor area, and we’ll come back with a fixed price, usually within one working day.
