Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Pink’s Home is a house cleaning business based near Llanelli, covering Carmarthenshire, Swansea and Pembrokeshire. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website and what we do with it.

Who we are

Pink’s Home is run by Shakira Williams and Kins Brown. You can contact us at service@pinkshome.co.uk or on 07902 769352.

What we collect

When you fill in our quote form we collect your name, phone number, postcode, the type of clean you want, the number of bedrooms, any notes you add, and your email address if you choose to give it. If you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp we also hold whatever you send us.

Why we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, give you a price, arrange and carry out the clean, and take payment. We do this because you asked us to, and so that we can carry out the service you have requested. We will only contact you about your quote and your booking.

Who sees it

Quote form submissions are received through our website host, Netlify, and sent on to our email. We do not sell your details or use them for advertising. We share them only with people who need them to do the job, or if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

If you do not become a customer we delete your enquiry within 12 months. If you do, we keep your booking details for as long as we are cleaning for you and for up to 6 years afterwards for accounting and insurance records.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how we use it. Email us at service@pinkshome.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.
