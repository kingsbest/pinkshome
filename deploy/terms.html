Quotes and prices

Prices on this site are guides. We give a fixed quote once we know the size and condition of your home, usually from photos, and it will not change on the day unless the job turns out to be very different from what we were shown. Regular cleans are charged at £25 per hour with a two-hour minimum. Add-ons such as inside the oven, inside the fridge and interior windows are quoted separately.

We agree a date and an arrival window with you. You do not have to be home, but we need safe access, such as a key or key-safe code, and working water and electricity. Please tell us about pets, parking and anything fragile or off-limits.

Cancelling or changing a booking

Please give us at least 48 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or move a clean. We may charge for visits cancelled at shorter notice or where we cannot get in.

Payment

Payment is due after each clean by bank transfer or card, unless we have agreed something different with you.

Results

We will do our best to remove stains, limescale, mould and grease, but some marks cannot be fully removed without damaging the surface. We will tell you if we think this is likely.
